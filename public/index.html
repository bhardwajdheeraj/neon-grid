How to play
  1. Create a room and share its code or invite link.
  2. A second player joins from another device.
  3. Player X goes first. Take turns selecting an empty square.
  4. Get three marks in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line to win.
  5. If all nine squares fill without a winner, it's a draw.
  6. After a finished game, select Play again to start a new round.
